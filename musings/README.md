This section contains a hodge-podge of ideas and directions to follow. As they turn out to be promising these are moved to the relevant section, eventually finding their way into a skill or an agent. 


There are a few main directions to pursue:

* How to extract knowledge from existing resource (e.g. code base, literature, good practices, textbooks, blogs, ...) that is tailored to the target application domain.
* How to organize knowledge
* How to retrieve a corpus of knowledge to be injected into the context ahead of addressing the ask from the user. 
* How to process the question, including how to decide what can the agent assume (both in the KB and in general knowledge), when to ask for precision, how to break the question into smaller parts, ...)
