# Engineer residuals: request interpretation and authorization

Status: under review; extracted 2026-11-24. No behavior is adopted by this RFC.

Source: [archived Engineer](../../graveyard/skills/engineer/SKILL.md).
Baseline: [Gerald](../../skills/gerald/SKILL.md) and its dimension, stage, and retention references.

Engineer is an early Gerald, not a separate engineering role. Gerald already covers outcome framing, consequential gaps, scope, depth, user overrides, routine reversible choices, and unfinished obligations. Those are not new proposals here.

## Residuals for review

| Source section | Not explicit in Gerald | Review proposal / unresolved choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish the request — What / How / Why | A compact checklist distinguishing the ask, concepts, user-supplied method, motivation, and constraints; preserve suggestions/examples as intent breadcrumbs rather than silently treating them as requirements | Consider a reference checklist for consequential ambiguity. Avoid making every request an interview. These intent breadcrumbs differ from retrieval breadcrumbs. |
| Rephrase | Explicit user-visible restatement before substantive work and after material reinterpretation | Consider whether Gerald’s invocation outcome summary suffices or a targeted restatement rule adds value. |
| Plan and obtain approval | Blanket advance approval for coding and potentially costly external research; explicit reuse of prior authorization | Review an authorization policy separately. Do not reinstate blanket gates or reopen authorization already supplied by the user. Plan/spec versus Plan/detailed already has a narrower transition boundary in Gerald. |
| Handle the unexpected | Explicit escalation when execution cost/complexity materially exceeds the communicated scope; concise plan amendments instead of wholesale restatement | Consider a material scope/resource-change rule, distinct from routine implementation choices and optional discoveries. |
| Active-instruction canary | Refer to oneself as “the Agent” | Historical convention, with no proposed reinstatement. Gerald already has a visible framing card, and neither signal proves instruction adherence. |

## Open questions

Which checklist details improve interpretation without duplicating Investigator? What constitutes a material resource increase? Which authorization controls belong in host/project policy rather than a portable skill?

Recordkeeping coverage is governed by Gerald. This RFC adds no mandatory records and no knowledge-promotion authority.
